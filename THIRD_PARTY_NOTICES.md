# Third-Party Notices

This repository is a metadata-first catalog. Most third-party projects are referenced by URL and are **not vendored**.

- Every referenced project retains its own copyright, license, trademark, and usage terms.
- A license label in `data/catalog.json` is discovery metadata, not a substitute for the upstream license text.
- Before copying code, SVG, screenshots, documentation, or other assets, re-check the exact upstream revision and its LICENSE/NOTICE files.
- Resources marked `Unknown`, `Mixed`, or `review-required` are link-only until reviewed.
- Brand icon licenses do not automatically grant trademark rights.

When vendored assets are added later, place exact license/notice copies and provenance records under `third_party/`.
